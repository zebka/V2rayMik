V2rayMik — RouterOS import generator

Choose your router and storage, then copy one line. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Storage

Auto-detect tries usb1–3, sata, nvme, pcie, mmc/sd, nand, then flash, in that order. Find your disk name with /file print where type=directory or Winbox Files.

A USB stick is usually mounted as usb1-part1. Use -part1 for a formatted stick, and the plain name for an unformatted one.

Access

The panel is reached at http://<router-lan-ip>:<port>/, from any device on your LAN.

Must be unique on the router. Existing containers keep their own name on upgrade.

Registry

In Iran the official registry is often unreachable. The installer tries it first, then falls back to arvancloud, once.

Use "No" only for mirrors such as arvancloud or iranserver. Keep "Yes" for the official registry.

Result

...
